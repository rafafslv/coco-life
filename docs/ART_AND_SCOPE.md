# Coco Life — first playable slice

## Design direction
The prototype translates project ideas into original lightweight 2D/2.5D vector art: a colorful friendly world, rounded buildings, an expressive avatar, vehicles and approachable room interiors. It takes broad genre inspiration from open-ended life-play and avatar-world exploration, without reusing Toca Boca or PK XD characters, logos, maps, UI art or other protected assets.

## World places
- Cloud Home — home and cozy objects
- Coco Café — cake, cupcake and hot chocolate props
- Style Studio — mirror, outfit rack and accessories
- Petal Park — flowers, butterflies and playground
- Speed Garage — car, wheels and tools
- Play Plaza — ferris wheel, present and balloons

The neighborhood is a ring. Rotate with on-screen arrows or the R key, then tap a building to enter. Inside, tap an object to interact and collect five Coco coins.

## Avatar
The first wardrobe has the requested mint green + white and sunshine yellow + white palettes, with three hair color options. Choices persist locally.

## Controls and safety
- Desktop: WASD / arrow keys, mouse click, R, E / Space, Escape
- Touch: tap-to-move and on-screen virtual joystick; large tap targets
- No account, free-text chat, uploads, tracking, analytics or multiplayer
